A Trusted partner in learning

StudyRescue provides their students with text books, notes/syllabus and past papers to help them understand what is expected from them. This makes it easy for students to stay organized and manage their time - helping students plan their study time and avoid last-minute stress. The syllabus outlines how grades are calculated (exams, assignments, participation, etc.), so students know what to focus on. Students can prepare ahead - With a schedule of lessons and readings, students can read in advance and come to class more prepared

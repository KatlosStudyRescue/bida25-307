Services

Our services are tailored to meet each learner's unique needs and curriculum requirements. At Katlo's StudyRescue we offer:

PSLE Tutoring

Focused support for Primary School Leaving Examination students. Lessons strengthen core subject understanding, improve exam techniques, and build a strong foundation for secondary education.

We tutor core subjects such as Science, Mathematics, English, Setswana, Social studies, Agriculture, Religious and Moral education. The price for each subject is P150.

JCE Tutoring

Targeted tutoring to help Junior Certificate Examination students grasp challenging topics, improve problem-solving skills, and prepare effectively for assessments and examinations.

We offer core subject tutoring only (No optional subjects) such as Science, Mathematics, English, Setswana, Moral education, Social studies, and Agriculture. Each subject going for P250.

BGCSE Tutoring

Comprehensive tutoring aligned with the Botswana General Certificate of Secondary Education syllabus. We focus on content mastery, exam preparation, and developing critical thinking skills.

We tutor subjects such as Science Double Award or Triple Science, Mathematics (includes paper 3), English, Setswana, Agriculture, Computer Studies, Business and Humanities (Commerce, Accounting, Business Studies,Social Studies, History, Geography, and Development studies). Each subject is P250.

IGCSE Tutoring

Structured and curriculum-aligned tutoring for Internatinal General Certificate of Secondary Education students. Sessions emphasize clear explanations, exam strategies, and consistent academic progress across subjects.

The subjects we tutor are, Sciences (Biology, Chemistry, Physics/ Double award), Mathematics (including Additional Mathematics), English (First/second Language), Setswana, ICT, Computer Science, Business and Humanities (Accounting, Business Studies, Econimics, History, Geography, and Development Studies). Each subject costs P300.

Study Assistence & Skills Development

We teach students how to study effectively, not just what to study. This service includes: Personalized study methods, Time management skills, Note-taking techniques, Exam preparation strategies, and Building confidence and independent learning habits. This service is ideal for students who struggle with organization, motivation, or finding study methods that work best for them. The sessions are free of charge

Services

Our services are tailored to meet each learner's unique needs and curriculum requirements.At Katlo's StudyRescue we offer:

PSLE Tutoring

Focused support for Primary School Leaving Examination students. Lessons strengthen core subject understanding, improve exam techniques, and build a strong foundation for secondary education.

JCE Tutoring

Targeted tutoring to help Junior Certificate Examination students grasp challenging topics, improve problem-solving skills, and prepare effectively for assessments and examinations.

BGCSE Tutoring

Comprehensive tutoring aligned with the Botswana General Certificate of Secondary Education syllabus. We focus on content mastery, exam preparation, and developing critical thinking skills.

IGCSE Tutoring

Structured and curriculum-aligned tutoring for IGCSE students. Sessions emphasize clear explanations, exam strategies, and consistent academic progress across subjects.

Study Assistence & Skills Development

We teach students how to study effectively, not just what to study. This service includes:

Personalized study methods

Time management skills

Note-taking techniques

Exam preparation strategies

Building confidence and independent learning habits

This service is ideal for students who struggle with organization, motivation, or finding study methods that work best for them.
